# Amanah Care Circle

Create Amanah, an accessible support and communication platform connecting children with neurodivergent, learning, and psychological conditions to parents, educators, and qualified therapists across the UAE–India partnership.

### Visual & Color Theme

- Warm Honey-Cream palette:
  - Golden Warm Cream (#F7EEDB) for glare-reducing, paper-like background warmth
  - Soft Warm Linen (#FAF5EC) for cards and reading surfaces
  - Calming Lavender (#C7BCDF) for soothing primary accents, feelings, and interactive touchpoints
  - Warm Blue (#7E9AB8) for voice messages, clinical guidance, and reassurance
  - Muted Sage (#BACABE) for positive cues and encouragement badges
  - Muted Slate (#313A46) for soft, high-contrast readable typography (no jarring pure black)
- Sensory-friendly design: generous spacing, rounded corners, soft transitions, zero aggressive flashes or sudden loud sounds.

### Onboarding & Adaptive Mode Selector ("Select What You Have")

Start with a welcoming, accessible onboarding flow and persistent header selector where users choose what describes them:

1. Role Selection:
   - Child / Student (launches child-friendly, low-pressure expression tools)
   - Parent / Guardian (family insight stream, daily emotional check-ins, home strategies)
   - Educator / School (classroom accommodation tips, learning need flags, student status)
   - Qualified Therapist (clinical guidance feed, private notes, cross-border async review)
2. Condition & Accommodation Selector (supports selecting one or multiple from 16 profiles):
   - Neurodevelopmental & Focus:
     - ADHD (low-distraction view, one-tap voice memos, focus micro-tasks, visual timers)
     - Executive Functioning (visual step-by-step task breakdowns, memory prompts)
     - Autism Spectrum / ASD (predictable schedules, pictogram tap-to-talk, sensory overload alerts)
     - Sensory Processing / SPD (extra-muted sensory themes, sound spike filters, overload signal)
   - Learning & Processing:
     - Dyslexia (adaptive weighted font such as Lexend, line-focus reading ruler, syllable spacing, text-to-speech)
     - Dysgraphia (speech-to-text dictation, icon-based communication cards)
     - Dyscalculia (visual math representations, symbol counters, math anxiety relief)
   - Anxiety, OCD & Trauma:
     - Anxiety & Social Phobia (zero-pressure async communication, guided breathing, 'I need a break' signal)
     - OCD & Intrusive Thoughts (grounding reframes, calming exposure pacing, reassurance check-ins)
     - PTSD & Trauma Recovery (safe-harbor mode, trigger logs, 5-4-3-2-1 sensory grounding)
     - Panic Disorder (SOS emergency calming button, guided slow-respiration pulse)
   - Mood & Emotional Regulation:
     - Depression & Low Mood (safe feelings journal, micro-wins, direct therapist ping)
     - Emotional Dysregulation / DMDD / ODD (cooldown thermometer, anger de-escalation, choice boards)
     - Bipolar & Mood Cycles (energy/sleep rhythm logs, proactive caregiver trends)
   - Speech & Tic Conditions:
     - Selective Mutism (tap-to-speak soundboard, text avatar relay, zero verbal demand)
     - Tourette & Tic Disorders (stress-calm zone, teacher 'tic surge' status badge)

### Child Expression Studio

- Children can express feelings, communicate difficulties, and request help through text, voice messages, and visual tools without face-to-face pressure.
- Interactive emotion dial / mood cards (happy, overwhelmed, confused, tired, anxious, proud).
- Voice memo recorder with audio waveform and optional transcription.
- Dyslexia-friendly canvas with text-to-speech read aloud and reading ruler.

### Support Circle & Caregiver Portals

- Parent insight stream: view child's check-ins, therapist recommendations, and teacher notes.
- Educator classroom desk: view individual accommodation summaries and daily support cues.
- Therapist clinical hub: review voice notes and check-ins, provide professional guidance, and share asynchronous developmental feedback.

### UAE–India Cross-Border Partnership & SDG Impact

- Showcase the bilateral partnership:
  - UAE: funding, healthcare infrastructure, school implementation, technological innovation.
  - India: digital talent, startups, clinical and therapeutic expertise.
- Transparent consent and child data protection framework for cross-border collaboration.
- SDG Impact Dashboard:
  - SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being (improved access to developmental and mental health support)
  - SDG 4: Quality Education (inclusive learning and individual educational accommodations)
  - SDG 17: Partnerships for the Goals (cross-border collaboration between families, schools, and specialists)
